Built for regulated products

When "the AI did it" isn't an acceptable answer

In fintech, KYC, legal and health, AI features carry three extra expectations: a human can intervene, AI content is distinguishable from human content, and decisions are traceable. Most agent UI kits treat these as edge cases. agentic treats them as the core.

  • KYC & onboarding — approval gates and provenance on every extracted field.
  • Fintech dashboards — visible agent actions and execution traces, step by step.
  • Legal & health workflows — human-in-the-loop before anything is committed.

agentic gives you the UI building blocks — compliance itself is your product's job.
